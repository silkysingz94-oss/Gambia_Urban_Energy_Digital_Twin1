INTERPRETATION & LIMITATIONS
Select an administrative area and run the analysis.
WorldPop is a modelled gridded population estimate. Household totals are estimated using GBoS mean household size, not observed enumeration totals. OSM features are incomplete where mapping is incomplete. PURA national sales figures cannot be distributed to districts without verified local consumption records. Scenario energy indicators are not measured electricity consumption or certified Positive Energy District results.
