INTERPRETATION & DATA LIMITATIONS
Select an administrative area and run the analysis.
Energy results are planning scenarios derived from user-defined assumptions. They are not measured electricity consumption, NAWEC customer data, grid simulation outputs, or a certified Positive Energy District assessment. OSM building and road data represent mapped features and may be incomplete. WorldPop values are modelled population estimates. GBoS household values are used only when actual census household attributes are found.
