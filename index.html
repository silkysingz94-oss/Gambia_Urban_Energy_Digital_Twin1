METHODOLOGY & DATA LIMITATIONS
Population: WorldPop Global2 R2025A, approximately 100 m population estimates. The selected polygon is submitted to the WorldPop API for the selected year.

Households: estimated by dividing population by the user-defined average household size. This is not a GBoS census household count.

Electricity: demand is modelled using the assumed annual electricity use per household and commercial/public-sector multiplier. It is not measured NAWEC consumption.

Solar: generation is estimated using installed PV capacity multiplied by an assumed annual solar yield. The monthly profile is illustrative, not a measured Gambia solar climatology.

Buildings: OpenStreetMap building footprints are incomplete and should not be interpreted as a complete building inventory.

PED: an annual PV-demand balance alone does not certify a Positive Energy District. A full assessment requires temporal demand and generation, imports/exports, storage losses, energy sectors and a clearly defined district boundary.
