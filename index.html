METHODOLOGY & LIMITATIONS
Population: WorldPop Global2 R2025A, approximately 100 m population data. The selected administrative geometry is sent to the WorldPop API.

Households: calculated by dividing population by the user-defined average household size. Not an official GBoS household count.

Energy demand: calculated from estimated households and user-defined electricity-use assumptions. Not NAWEC meter data.

Solar generation: installed PV capacity multiplied by the assumed annual solar yield. Not a measured solar-energy output.

Buildings: OSM footprints may be incomplete and do not represent a complete national building inventory.

PED assessment: annual energy balance alone does not establish Positive Energy District status. Hourly demand, generation, storage losses, grid imports and exports, and district energy boundaries are also required.
